Employee Data Analysis

An employee analytics project focused on understanding workforce performance, job satisfaction and employee attrition.

Project Overview

I cleaned and analyzed employee data to identify patterns related to performance, salary, satisfaction and attrition.

Tools Used

Python • Pandas • Google Colab • Power BI

What I Did

  • Cleaned and prepared the employee dataset.
  • Analyzed departments and job roles.
  • Examined salary and employee experience.
  • Analyzed performance ratings.
  • Studied job satisfaction and work-life balance.
  • Calculated the employee attrition rate.
  • Created visualizations and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Key Findings

  • The overall employee attrition rate was approximately 16.1%.
  • Job satisfaction was generally high among employees.
  • Employee attrition was analyzed against factors such as overtime and work-life balance.
  • Performance and salary patterns were also examined.

Project Outcome

The analysis turned employee data into useful information that can help organizations understand workforce trends, identify possible attrition factors and support better employee management decisions.
