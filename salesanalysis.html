Sales Data Analysis

A practical data analysis project focused on understanding sales performance, revenue, products and business trends.

Project Overview

This project involved cleaning, validating and analyzing sales data to discover useful business insights and support better decision-making.

Tools Used

Python • Pandas • Google Colab • Power BI

What I Did

  • Cleaned and prepared the sales dataset.
  • Checked missing values and duplicate records.
  • Validated important data fields.
  • Analyzed revenue and sales performance.
  • Compared product performance.
  • Created visualizations and a Power BI dashboard.
  • Extracted useful business insights.

Key Business Questions

  • Which products generate the most revenue?
  • How are sales changing over time?
  • Which areas or customer groups perform best?
  • What factors affect business performance?
  • What insights can help management make better decisions?

Project Outcome

The analysis transformed raw sales data into understandable information that can help a business monitor performance, identify trends and make data-driven decisions.
